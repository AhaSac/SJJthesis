## 中文

泵设计的数值计算正从平均水力性能评价扩展到压力脉动、激振力及噪声预测。本文选取近十年国内外代表性研究，区分高阶离散与高保真物理建模，梳理LES及混合RANS/LES方法在分离流动、动静干涉、叶顶空化和声振耦合中的应用。模型名称和平均扬程吻合不足以说明局部非定常量的精度，验证应围绕具体设计目标展开。最后讨论机器学习、格子玻尔兹曼及量子计算的适用边界，为常规泵CFD流程向非定常机理分析扩展提供参考。

关键词：泵设计；高精度数值模拟；大涡模拟；压力脉动；空化；水动力噪声。

## English

Numerical simulation in pump design increasingly addresses unsteady loads and noise in addition to mean hydraulic performance. This review examines representative studies published within the past decade, with emphasis on centrifugal pumps and flow mechanisms relevant to engineering design. It distinguishes the formal accuracy of spatial discretization from the physical fidelity of turbulence and multiphase models. Large-eddy simulation and hybrid RANS/LES approaches are discussed through applications involving flow separation, rotor–stator interaction, pressure fluctuations, fluid excitation forces, and tip-leakage cavitation. Hydrodynamic noise prediction introduces further requirements for resolving pressure sources and representing acoustic and structural transmission. Agreement in mean head alone therefore provides insufficient evidence for the accuracy of unsteady forces or broadband noise. Verification and validation should instead address the quantities of interest, including spatial and temporal sensitivity, statistical convergence, local flow measurements, and acoustic observations where applicable. Machine-learning-assisted solvers, lattice Boltzmann wall models, and quantum computing are considered as possible routes for reducing computational cost, while their demonstrated operating conditions and remaining limitations are retained. The review outlines a practical progression from routine performance screening to targeted, experimentally supported analysis of unsteady pump flows.

Keywords: pump design; high-accuracy numerical simulation; large-eddy simulation; pressure fluctuations; cavitation; hydrodynamic noise.
